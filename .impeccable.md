# BSA EPFL — Design Context

## Users

**Dual audience, roughly even weight:**

1. **Prospective EPFL students** — browsing to understand what BSA is, what they'd join, and what they'd get out of it.
2. **Potential sponsors and partners** — VCs, protocol teams, research partners, industry collaborators evaluating whether BSA is a credible partner for conferences, hackathons, research, or recruiting.

A future iteration (not in scope now) will split the FAQ into "I'm a student" and "I'm a sponsor" to serve each track explicitly. For now, both audiences share the same landing page, and the page must serve them simultaneously.

**Job to be done on landing:** convince the visitor — in under 30 seconds — that BSA is a serious, prolific organization doing exceptional work at EPFL. The current copy over-explains internal governance and process. The redesign must **lead with achievements**, not mechanics.

**Not a DAO.** BSA has a horizontal, flat hierarchy — but "DAO" is inaccurate and should be corrected wherever it appears in copy.

## Brand Personality

**Three-word voice:** cypherpunk, quietly prolific, detail-obsessed.

**Emotional target on exit:** "Shit — they're cool, they do so many things, each one so cool, and they actually care about every detail."

**Feelings to evoke:**
- **Serious people** doing real work
- **Real community** — warm through photography and event density, not through party copy
- **Cutting-edge research** — credibility for sponsors, aspiration for students
- **Cypherpunk heritage** — privacy, autonomy, open systems; a quiet ideological spine, not a slogan

**Feelings to avoid:**
- "Party I want to be at" — the fun side lives on social media, not here
- Corporate polish that reads as consulting-firm blandness
- "We're a DAO run by students" self-explanation — show output, not structure

## Aesthetic Direction

### Visual tone
Editorial density over airy minimalism. Dark, composed, information-rich. Rewards scrolling with evidence — events, people, startups, research — rather than stretching five ideas over five screens of whitespace.

### References (borrow the feel)

- **https://devinciblockchain.com/** — primary reference. The overall density, rhythm, and confidence of presentation.
- **https://www.tum-blockchain.com/** — secondary, partial. Useful structural ideas, but it visibly reads as AI-templated in places. Don't copy the tells.

### Anti-references (explicitly avoid)

- **https://www.kryptosphere.org/fr** — dated, cluttered, low-craft. If we start to resemble this, we've lost.
- Generic AI-made blockchain sites: neon cyan/purple gradients, glow-everywhere dark mode, stock isometric illustrations.

### Theme
**Dark mode only.** The brand lives in dark.

### Hard constraints (non-negotiable — these are the brand)

- **Color: BSA Blue `#152237`** — this exact value, used as the primary surface color across all BSA materials. Do not shift hue, lightness, or saturation. Build the palette *around* this blue.
- **Display font: Instrument Serif** — used for large headings like "Blockchain at EPFL" in the hero. This is the BSA display voice and stays. Note: this font is on Impeccable's default "reflex reject" list, but the user has overridden that — it is a deliberate brand choice.

### Open for redesign

- **Body font (currently Space Grotesk)** and **mono font (currently JetBrains Mono)** should be replaced. Both are on the AI monoculture reject list and have no brand lock. Pick fonts that pair with Instrument Serif and reinforce the "cypherpunk, prolific, detail-obsessed" voice.
- **Accent colors** around the BSA blue — currently the site uses zinc-gray accents and off-white text. There's room for a single bold accent or a refined neutral system that's tinted toward the BSA blue hue (so neutrals and brand blue feel like one system, not two).
- **Layout rhythm**, **photography treatment**, **component shapes**, **micro-interactions** — all open.

## Design Principles

1. **Showcase, don't explain.** Lead with outputs (conferences attended, hackathon prizes, startups incubated, research shipped, people involved). Internal mechanics are a footnote, not the pitch.
2. **Cypherpunk dignity.** Rigorous, a little mysterious, ideologically rooted — never corporate, never partyish, never cutesy.
3. **Proof through detail.** The visitor's takeaway should be "they care about every pixel." Typography, spacing, animation timing, and photography density all carry this weight. If a detail looks templated or generic, it's wrong.
4. **Brand-locked core, expressive edges.** BSA blue `#152237` and Instrument Serif are sacred. Everything else — secondary fonts, accent colors, layout, motion, component form — is open for bold, distinctive choices.
5. **One voice, two audiences.** Everything a student needs to feel invited and everything a sponsor needs to feel confident live in the same sentences. Don't split the voice; split the FAQ later.

## Accessibility & Internationalization

- **Language:** English only for now. French version is on a distant roadmap (Lausanne), so content structure should tolerate a future translation layer but doesn't need to be multilingual today.
- **No explicit WCAG target given.** Default to WCAG AA: contrast ratios on the dark blue must be checked, motion should respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, interactive targets meet 44×44 minimum on mobile.
